# Goodwill Equation Check

Diagnoses a client relationship against the goodwill balance: value delivered minus price charged, moved by every interaction.

A free plugin from KAIROS AI Systems — one focused tool that does one job well for a
small business: no suite to learn, no setup project, no account required beyond your
agent host.

## What it does

Surplus clients refer and forgive; zero-balance clients leave over trivia; negative clients actively dislike you. Every complaint is diagnosed against the balance first. Internal tool — never client-facing language.

## How to use it

Add this plugin to a compatible agent host (Cursor, Grok Bot, or another
agent-plugins host), then ask in plain language, for example:

> Use the Goodwill Equation Check to help me: diagnoses a client relationship against the goodwill balance: value delivered minus price charged, moved by every interaction — I'll paste the details.

The plugin works from the facts you give it — your services, your prices, your
records. It never invents a number, a policy, or a result; anything it projects is
labelled an estimate with its basis. Drafts that would go to a customer stay drafts
until you approve them.
